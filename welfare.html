The welfare arm

Nobody in this house should carry a burden alone.

The welfare arm of the house exists for one reason: to make sure no family in our church or on our street carries a burden alone. Every gift given here becomes a bag of rice, a hospital bill paid, a school fee covered, a widow visited.

Prefer to give food or your time instead of money?

The welfare store is always short of a few things. Drop any of these at the church office during the week, or join the team on a Saturday delivery run.
