How it works

Simple, private, unhurried

    Our promise of confidentiality

    What you share stays with your counsellor. We do not keep it in church records, we do not discuss it in meetings, and we never contact your family on your behalf without permission. The only exception is an immediate risk to life; and even then we would tell you first.

    The team

    Real people, ready to listen

    Our counsellors are trained, accountable and part of the life of Power of God Zone. You will always know who you are meeting before the session begins.

    Book a session

    Request a counselling session

    Only the team sees this. Share as little or as much as you like to start.

    Confidential & free of charge

    What happens next

    • Your request goes only to the team inbox; never to a public list.
    • A counsellor contacts you within 48 hours to hear a little more.
    • You agree a time and place that feels safe for you, then meet.
    • Sessions continue for as long as they are useful, at no cost.

    In an emergency

    Counselling is not a crisis service. If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, please call the emergency line (112 in Nigeria) or go to the nearest hospital first; then let us know so we can stand with you.
